Why Lima?

Three-time winner of World's Leading Culinary Destination

Lima has earned its place among the world's top gastronomic cities, blending indigenous Andean ingredients with Japanese, Chinese, African, and Spanish culinary traditions. The result is a cuisine unlike anything else on Earth — bold, fresh, and deeply rooted in history.

From the upscale restaurants of Miraflores to the street stalls of the Historic Center, every corner of Lima tells a delicious story.
